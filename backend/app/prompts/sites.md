- Flights: Google Flights with a direct query URL, for example
  https://www.google.com/travel/flights?q=Flights%20from%20CAI%20to%20TYO%20on%202027-03-10%20through%202027-03-17
  (results load directly; extract "the list of flight results with prices").
- Hotels: Google Hotels search URL, for example
  https://www.google.com/travel/search?q=hotels%20in%20Tokyo%20from%202027-03-10%20to%202027-03-17
  or Booking.com: https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=Tokyo&checkin=2027-03-10&checkout=2027-03-17&group_adults=1&no_rooms=1
- Visa rules: Wikipedia "Visa requirements for <nationality> citizens", for example
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_requirements_for_Egyptian_citizens (extract the row for the destination country).
- Daily living / travel costs: https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/<country>/<city> (for example https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/japan/tokyo)
  or Numbeo: https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Tokyo
- Currency rates: https://www.x-rates.com/calculator/?from=USD&to=EGP&amount=1
- Product prices: Amazon search https://www.amazon.com/s?k=<query>, eBay https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=<query>
- General facts: Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<Title>
